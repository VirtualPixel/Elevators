# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- An upstairs and a basement on every level, built out of that level's own rooms and doors by the game's own generator calls, so they look like the level. Everyone in the lobby installs it (REPOLib for the networked buttons).
- The elevator: the Museum's car with a light inside, UP, DOWN and OPEN buttons on the walls, a call button at every floor, and a roof hatch you grab and swing. The buttons are the shop's pressable button on the Museum's wall plate. Doors open by button and on arrival, close on their own, hold for anyone or anything in them. It hums on the way, lands with a thud and a shudder, and a second press rides straight through to the next floor.
- The shaft: the Museum's own shaft walls and pipes with service lights, solid, a death pit at the bottom, and a speaker at every doorway so the car is heard from any floor.
- Museum rides its real shaft with the elevator room on every floor and sub floors as side stops. Manor at the top of the long stairs, McJannek Station in the stair corridor, Swiftbroom in the upstairs classroom, anything else in a plain room.
- Monsters: the car floor is on the navmesh wherever the car stands, so they walk in after you and ride along if they're inside when the doors shut. One that had you in its sights when the doors closed turns up on your floor a little later. Idle monsters walk to a call button, press it, and ride to another floor, more often than not yours. The Huntsman hears the shaft across his floor. One switch turns all of it off.
- The map: scroll through the floors, each drawn on its own with its name on the screen; it opens on your floor and follows you. The route to the extraction point or the truck leads to the nearest elevator across floors, and every elevator doorway is marked in its own colour.
- The ground floor is held to eight rooms; what a deep level would sprawl across goes on the other floors instead, extraction rooms included, so late levels grow up rather than out. One extraction point stands on a new floor whenever the level has two or more.
- Ground loot stays vanilla; the new floors get a modest share on top. The gap between floors is measured per level so nothing pokes through, and anyone stuck outside every room gets put back at the ground elevator.
