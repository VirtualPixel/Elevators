# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A second floor on every level, built out of that level's own rooms and doors by the game's own generator calls, so everyone in the lobby sees it. Only the host installs it.
- The elevator: the Museum's own car with a light and two buttons inside, a call button at each dock, and a dark shaft between floors. Doors open as you walk up, grab a button, and the car carries you there. Museum rides its own shaft, with the same elevator room on every floor, Manor at the top of the long stairs, Arctic and Wizard in their stair rooms. Other levels get one in a plain room. Players without the mod get tumbled up instead, the way the game pulls you out of the void.
- Optional basement (crouch on the lift to go down).
- The ground floor keeps its normal loot; the new floors get their own share on top. The quota grows with it like any other valuable.
- Monsters keep to the ground floor unless you say otherwise.
- The gap between floors is measured per level from the tallest and deepest rooms, so nothing pokes through. End up outside every room and the lift catches you.
- A HUD line pointing at the nearest elevator, for everyone with the mod.
