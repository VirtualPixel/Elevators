# Changelog

## Unreleased

- A level point rides on the car floor, so roaming monsters wander into the lift on their own instead of only when chased in.

- Level one gets a floor. Five rooms were one short of the bar, so the first level of every run was plain.

- The call button goes on the wall beside the doors, like a real elevator, and only falls back to a post when that wall has no room. It no longer ends up inside a wall or a shelf (Arctic Cross Corridors, Manor Master Bedroom).
- The doorway marks on the map line up with the elevator doors.
- Floors above and below get a different room at the elevator, so stepping out doesn't look like the room you just left.
- The car dings when it lands, waits a couple of seconds, and the doors open slowly.
- More elevator sound: creaks in the shaft, a knock as the car passes a floor, the doors settling, and a motor that sounds a little different every trip.
- Monsters anywhere on a floor go and take the elevator, not only ones already near it. They head for the floor the cart is on first, then a floor with players.
- A monster that reaches a landing while the car is elsewhere calls it, one call in a while so the car isn't passed around.
- The shaft has a solid floor. A head that falls down it goes back to the truck, and anything else that lands there is handled the way the game handles things that fall out of the level.
- Looking back out of the car, the wall around the doorway is there instead of see-through.
- Fixed a lost extraction point when the elevator's first wall was blocked and the floors were planned again.
