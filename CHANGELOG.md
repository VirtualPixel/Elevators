# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A second floor on every level, built out of that level's own rooms and doors by the game's own generator calls, so everyone in the lobby sees it. Only the host installs it.
- The lift: stand on the pad and the host tumbles you up to the matching pad on the floor above, the same move the game uses to pull you out of the void. Museum uses the elevator landing, Manor the top of the long stairs, Arctic and Wizard their stair rooms. Other levels get a pad in a plain room.
- Optional basement (crouch on the lift to go down).
- Extra valuables on the new floors, on top of the game's normal spread. The quota grows with them like any other valuable.
- Monsters keep to the ground floor unless you say otherwise.
- The gap between floors is measured per level from the tallest and deepest rooms, so nothing pokes through. Fall between floors and the lift catches you.
- Moon-phase style popup on load telling the host where the lift is.
