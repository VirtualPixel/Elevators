# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A second floor on every level, built out of that level's own rooms and doors by the game's own generator calls. Everyone in the lobby installs it (it needs REPOLib for the networked buttons).
- The elevator: the Museum's own car with a light and two buttons inside, a call button at each dock, and a dark shaft between floors with a drop at the bottom. Doors open as you walk up, grab a button, and the car carries you there. The buttons are the game's shop button, grab area and all. The roof hatch is a door you grab and swing. Museum rides its own shaft, with the same elevator room on every floor, Manor at the top of the long stairs, Arctic and Wizard in their stair rooms. Other levels get one in a plain room. Players without the mod get tumbled up instead, the way the game pulls you out of the void.
- The car lands with a thud and a shudder and the doors wait a beat. On the Museum the call button sits on the room's own wall plate and the landing doors slide behind the wall.
- The map: scroll through the floors while it is open, each drawn on its own with its name on the screen. It opens on your floor and follows you between floors. Its route to the extraction point or the truck leads to the nearest elevator when that is on another floor.
- Monsters follow you upstairs by the game's own respawn shuffle, an elevator leaving or landing draws whatever is nearby to the shaft, and one that had you in its sights when the doors shut turns up on your new floor a little later. Every so often an idle monster rides the elevator to another floor, and every dock carries the shaft's sound so you hear it coming.
- Call opens the doors when the car is there, a second press mid-trip queues the next floor, and a press the car can't honour gets the upgrade stand's refusal.
- Optional basement (crouch on the lift to go down).
- The ground floor keeps its normal loot; the new floors get a modest share on top (30 percent by default). The quota grows with it like any other valuable.
- Monsters keep to the ground floor unless you say otherwise.
- The gap between floors is measured per level from the tallest and deepest rooms, so nothing pokes through. End up outside every room and the lift catches you.
- A HUD line pointing at the nearest elevator, for everyone with the mod.
